Desa Punggul - Monthly Report

SECTION 1: SOCIAL DOMAIN
Elderly residents above 60 years old rarely use digital services.
The village population is approximately 3,401 inhabitants.
Social conflicts occur when water access is limited.

SECTION 2: ECONOMY DOMAIN
If household income exceeds 5 million rupiah, families own smartphones.
The village has 523 small businesses.
Market access is limited in the north district.

SECTION 3: INFRASTRUCTURE DOMAIN
If water consumption exceeds 300 liters per day, the village reports a leak.
The north district lacks clean water access.
Road infrastructure connects 5 hamlets.

SECTION 4: HEALTH DOMAIN
When malaria cases exceed 10 per month, health alerts are issued.
The health center serves all 5 hamlets.
Vaccination rates are above 85%.

SECTION 5: CULTURE & ARTS DOMAIN
Traditional ceremonies occur during harvest season.
Local art groups perform at community events.
Cultural heritage includes traditional weaving techniques.
